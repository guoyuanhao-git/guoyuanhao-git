# University seals

Official university artwork, displayed here to identify educational affiliations.

- Peking University: [Visual Identity Office downloads](https://vim.pku.edu.cn/xzzq/), “标志(圆形校徽).zip”. The red seal is used on light backgrounds; the official reversed seal is used on dark backgrounds.
- Wuhan University: [University identity](https://www.whu.edu.cn/xxgk/wdbs.htm), “校徽” download. The standard two-colour seal is used on light backgrounds; the official reversed seal is used on dark backgrounds.

The PNG files are unchanged from the official downloads. All university marks belong to their respective institutions.
